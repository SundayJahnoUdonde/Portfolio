03 / Selected Work

Case studies

Three representative engagements, written as problem, action and measured result.

Case 01Power

Hybrid solar & backup system, residential

Problem
Six to eight hours of grid outage per day, with a generator running most evenings and fuel cost rising monthly.
Action
Full load audit of every circuit, then a 6.6 kWp array with a 5 kVA hybrid inverter and 10 kWh storage, installed with labelled distribution and monitored battery state.
Result
18–22 hours of autonomy per charge; generator runtime cut from roughly six hours to under one hour per day.
Load audit PV sizing Hybrid inverter
Case 02Automation

Three-phase motor control panel rebuild

Problem
A workshop panel with undocumented circuits and no phase-failure protection; a single fault could stop the whole line for hours.
Action
Rebuilt the panel with correctly rated contactors and overloads, added phase-failure protection, and produced a circuit schedule fixed inside the door.
Result
Fault identification reduced from hours of tracing to minutes; unplanned stoppages down by approximately 70%.
Three-phase Protection Documentation
Case 03Systems

Technical academy lab & learning portal

Problem
Training depended on verbal instruction with no bench capacity, no record of learner progress and no reusable material.
Action
Designed a twelve-bench electrical and electronics laboratory, and built a full-stack portal covering enrolment, course material and module assessment.
Result
150+ learners onboarded, 87% module completion, and paper-based enrolment and grading removed entirely.
Lab design Full-stack Assessment
